Signal Scoring
🗳️
Confidence-weighted vote — all 20 strategies vote BUY / SELL / HOLD each bar. Each vote's weight is its own confidence; HOLD counts in the denominator, so confidence = winning-side weight ÷ all voters. The result must clear the per-symbol minimum to act.
✅
Multi-bar confirmation — borderline entries (within ~0.10 of the minimum) also need the previous bar to agree before firing.
↔️
Asymmetric exit — an open position exits once SELL pressure reaches 70% of the normal threshold: quicker to protect, slower to enter.

Entry Filters
📉
Regime filter — blocks new BUY entries when ADX is below the threshold, indicating a ranging/choppy market with no clear trend.
🔗
Correlation filter — skips a BUY if an open position is already highly correlated (Pearson r ≥ 0.85) with the incoming coin, avoiding doubling up on the same move.
🕒
MTF 15m alignment — inspects the 15m candles inside the 12h bar; blocks BUY when the short-term trend disagrees (align score < 0.50).
⏱️
4h momentum — EMA(8/21) + RSI(14) on 4h candles; blocks BUY when the 4h momentum score < 0.45.
🐻
Macro bear filter — when BTC trades below its 200-EMA, new position size is halved.
🌧️
Bear policy — on BTC entering a bear trend, all open positions close and new entries are blocked until the regime clears.
👑
BTC dominance gate — blocks alt BUYs when BTC.D is >1pp above its 7-day average (capital rotating into BTC).
😱
Fear & Greed — sentiment tweaks the entry bar: extreme greed (>80) demands more conviction, extreme fear (<20) loosens it (contrarian).
Position Sizing
🎚️
Confidence sizing — bet scales with conviction: 0.6× at low confidence up to 1.5× at full confidence.
📐
Regime sizing — ADX-based: strong trends (ADX ≥ 25) sized up 1.3×, choppy ranges (ADX < 15) sized down 0.5×.
Risk Protections
⚡
Break-even — once price rises to the trigger %, the stop loss moves to entry so the position can never close at a loss.
🔻
Trailing stop — stop loss follows the price upward, locking in gains as the trade runs in our favour.
🛑
Fixed stop — standard stop loss at a fixed % below entry price.
🧯
Weekly drawdown breaker — a −10% portfolio loss in any 7-day window pauses new entries for 72h.
⏳
Position aging exit — positions open 14 bars (~7 days) without hitting TP/SL are closed to free up capital.
